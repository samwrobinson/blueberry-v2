Sauna Passes

Sauna at Blueberry Lake XC

This year, we are excited to expand the nordic experience beyond skiing and snowshoeing! Our neighbords Jesse and Alicia are currently building an eight-person trailside sauna for us! The sauna will be open on weekends, holidays, and weekdays from 4 - 7 PM, or anytime by booking ahead for parties of two or more. Sauna appointments must be scheduled at least 1 hr in advance, and are booked in 45 minute increments.

Sauna Season will run from December 20th - March 15th or until whenever the ski season closes, whichever comes later.

Monthly passes include unlimited sauna use for a 30-day period, and only existing customers who have purchased a season's ski passes are eligible for the discounted sauna add-on pricing.

Single session saunas are only available for purchase in person or by phone.
